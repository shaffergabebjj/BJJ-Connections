Training Resources

Drilling ideas, conditioning concepts, and training tools to help you grow as a grappler. Save your progress as you go.

Solo Drills

Exercises you can do without a partner to improve your movement and conditioning.

Hip Escapes (Shrimping)

Mobility · 5 min

Practice hip movement (shrimping) on the ground to build guard retention and escape mechanics. Focus on smooth, controlled movement.

Bridging

Power · 5 min

Bridge up onto your shoulders to build the explosive hip power needed for mount escapes and reversals. Keep feet flat, drive through heels.

Technical Stand-up

Movement · 5 min

Practice the technical stand-up motion to build muscle memory for getting up safely from the ground. Protect your head and maintain balance.

Forward & Backward Rolls

Mobility · 3 min

Practice rolling forward and backward on the mat to improve comfort with falling and positional transitions.

Shadow Grappling

Technique · 10 min

Move through techniques and sequences without a partner — visualize grips, foot placement, and transitions. Like shadow boxing for BJJ.

Grip Hangs

Grip Strength · 3 sets

Hang from a pull-up bar to build grip endurance. Try alternating between full grip and pinch grips. Build up gradually.

Partner Drilling Ideas

Repetition-based drills to build muscle memory with a training partner.

Guard Pass Defense

Defense · 5 rounds

Partner attempts to pass while you work on guard retention. Reset after each pass or successful retention. Focus on framing and hip movement.

Escape Repetition

Escapes · 10 reps each

Partner puts you in a bad position (mount, side control, back). Practice your escape, reset, and repeat. Switch positions after each set.

Positional Sparring

Game Plan · 3 min rounds

Start from a specific position and spar from there. Examples: start in half guard, start in side control, start in closed guard.

Submission Chain Drilling

Offense · 10 min

Practice a sequence of submissions that flow from one to the next — for example: armbar → triangle → omoplata from guard.

Sweep Timing

Offense · 5 rounds

Partner stands in your guard with a specific posture. Practice timing your sweeps based on their weight distribution and reactions.

Takedown Entries

Stand-up · 5 min

Practice grip fighting and setting up takedown entries. Focus on level changes, footwork, and hand fighting.

Warm-Up Ideas

Get your body ready for training with these BJJ-specific warm-ups.

  • Jogging around the mat — 2 minutes to raise heart rate
  • Forward and backward rolling — 1 minute
  • Shrimping (hip escapes) — 1 minute
  • Bridging — 1 minute
  • Technical stand-ups — 1 minute
  • Spider-man walks — 1 minute
  • Light stretching — focus on hips, shoulders, neck
  • Shadow grappling — 2 minutes of flowing through positions

Strength & Conditioning Concepts

General concepts for grapplers. This is not medical or professional fitness advice — consult a qualified strength coach for personalized programming.

Disclaimer: The information below is general educational content, not medical advice. Always consult a qualified professional before starting any exercise program. Train under supervision when learning new movements.

Grip Training

Dead hangs, farmer's carries, and gi pull-ups build the grip endurance essential for gi grappling. Start with 3 sets of 15-30 second hangs.

Core Strength

Planks, Russian twists, and hanging leg raises build the core stability needed for bridges, guard retention, and explosive movement.

Explosive Power

Plyometric exercises like box jumps and medicine ball throws help develop the explosive movement needed for takedowns and sweeps.

Cardiovascular Endurance

Better conditioning means you can train longer and harder. Running, rowing, and swimming build the aerobic base that supports BJJ performance.

Mobility

Hip and shoulder mobility are crucial for BJJ. Regular stretching, yoga, or mobility drills help prevent injuries and improve performance.

Neck Strength

Neck bridges and isometric holds build neck strength important for defending chokes and maintaining posture. Build gradually and carefully.
