How to play

16 words. 4 hidden groups of 4. Tap a word to select it, tap 3 more, then hit Submit.

Guess right and that group locks in. Guess wrong and you lose one of your 4 mistakes. Get all 4 groups before you run out of mistakes.

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Try Training mode to practice by belt-level difficulty, or Archive to replay past days.
