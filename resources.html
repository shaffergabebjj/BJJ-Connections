The Belt System

Belt progression in BJJ is based on time, knowledge, and competition performance. Requirements vary by association.

BeltTypical DurationFocus
White1-2 yearsFundamentals: basic positions, escapes, and submissions
Blue2-3 yearsBuilding a game: developing a personal style and refining technique
Purple2-4 yearsAdvanced techniques: guard specialization, competition strategy
Brown1-3 yearsMastery: refining details, teaching ability, competition at high level
BlackLifetimeExpertise: deep understanding of all aspects of BJJ
Note: Timeframes are general guidelines only. Each association (IBJJF, CBJJ, etc.) has its own requirements. Belt progression depends on attendance, technique, competition results, and instructor assessment.
